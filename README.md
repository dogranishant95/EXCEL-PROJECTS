## ⚡ 1. UPI Ecosystem: Digital Payments & Fraud Analytics Dashboard

### 📌 Business Overview & Problem Statement
Unified Payments Interface (UPI) ecosystem mein daily million transactions route hote hain jisme payment success rates, app market share dominance, bank settlement loads, aur fraudulent transaction patterns ko monitor karna mandatory hota hai. Yeh dashboard transactional bottlenecks, fraud hotspots, aur volume patterns ko real-time analyze karta hai.

### 📸 Dashboard Architecture & Visuals
![UPI Transactions Dashboard](upi%20dashboard%20image.jpeg)

### 📈 Core Business KPIs & Metrics Tracked
- **Total Processed Volume:** 5,02,887 Transactions
- **Total Settlement Value:** ₹44.25 Cr Gross Transaction Value (GTV)
- **Customer Incentive Load:** ₹34.63 L Cashback disbursed
- **Transaction Reliability:** 91.00% Success Rate (7.0% Failed | 2.0% Pending)
- **Risk & Fraud Detection:** 17,089 Suspected Fraudulent Transactions flagged for auditing

### 🔍 Deep-Dive Insights & Exploratory Dimensions
- **App Dominance (Market Share Breakdown):** PhonePe leads transaction volume at **49.8%**, followed by Google Pay (**21.3%**), Paytm (**14.3%**), Amazon Pay (**4.9%**), BHIM (**3.8%**), and CRED (**3.0%**).
- **Settlement Bank Workload:** HDFC Bank, State Bank of India (SBI), and Kotak Mahindra Bank absorb the highest transaction traffic.
- **Geographic Dispersion:** State-wise heatmap highlights Maharashtra (₹4.54 Cr), Karnataka (₹4.51 Cr), and Delhi (₹4.48 Cr) as the top payment generators.
- **Temporal Heatmap & Hourly Surge:** 24-hour frequency analysis isolates peak settlement hours and day-of-week volume distributions.
- **Transaction Types:** Deep segment tracking between Peer-to-Merchant (P2M ~ ₹18.52 Cr) and Peer-to-Peer (P2P ~ ₹8.77 Cr).

### 🛠️️ Technical Implementation & Excel Architecture
- **Interactive Multi-Level Slicers:** City, Gender, Merchant Category (Fuel, Grocery, Credit Card, Healthcare), and Merchant Names.
- **Advanced Formulas Used:** Dynamic calculations using nested `SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `INDEX/MATCH`, and conditional logic.
- **Visual Design Principles:** Dark/Emerald green financial theme, customized donut & bar visuals, gridline-free executive card layouts.

## 🍔 2. Swiggy Food Delivery & Regional Sales Intelligence Dashboard

### 📌 Business Overview & Problem Statement
Hyperlocal food delivery marketplace models require tight monitoring of Average Order Value (AOV), delivery partner routing loads, restaurant partner performance, and consumer dietary preferences across fiscal quarters. Yeh dashboard complete e-commerce food delivery sales funnel ko summarize karta hai.

### 📸 Dashboard Architecture & Visuals
![Swiggy Dashboard](swiggy%20dashboard%20image.jpeg)

### 📈 Core Business KPIs & Metrics Tracked
- **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV):** ₹53.01 Million Total Sales
- **Customer Satisfaction:** 4.34 / 5.0 Average Rating (Across 5.59M rating submissions)
- **Basket Size Economics:** ₹268.51 Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Order Fulfillment Volume:** 197.43K Total Successful Orders

### 🔍 Deep-Dive Insights & Exploratory Dimensions
- **Dietary Preference Split:** Non-Veg orders dominate revenue contribution with **64% (₹33.9M)** vs Veg with **36% (₹19.1M)**.
- **Quarterly Sales Breakdown:** Q1 (₹19.7M Sales, 73.1K Orders) and Q2 (₹19.9M Sales, 74.2K Orders) show consistent demand, tracking seasonal variance in Q3.
- **Top Revenue Generating Hubs:** Bengaluru leads city rankings with ₹5.46M, followed by Lucknow (₹3.12M), Mumbai (₹3.02M), and Hyderabad (₹3.02M).
- **Order Surge Trends:** Clear weekend spikes on Saturday (₹7.78M) and Sunday (₹7.64M) reflecting consumer leisure ordering behaviors.
- **Geographic Density:** Heatmap geo-mapping showing order volumes across Indian state borders.

### 🛠️ Technical Implementation & Excel Architecture
- **Navigation UX:** Custom dashboard navigation sidebar (Dashboard, Analysis, Data) using Shapes & Hyperlinks.
- **Dynamic Slicing:** Multi-field filtering across Months (Jan–Sep), Food Categories, and Individual Restaurant Partners.
- **Formulas & Data Aggregations:** Dynamic arrays, multi-criteria KPI aggregation, automated data labels, and custom color-graded thematic formatting.
